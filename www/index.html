GATERUSH

Hold the gates. Rush the keep.

PICK YOUR RIDE

How to play
Fly
Drag anywhere to move (or WASD). You fire automatically. Space fires the Big One. Esc pauses.
Gates
Gates you hold multiply your blobs and stop enemy bullets. Bullets and minions wear them down; a red gate multiplies the keep's minions instead. Fire through it to take it back, or send the Big One to retake it at once.
Bullets
Your blobs block plain bullets one for one. Outlined bullets pass straight through your stream, so dodge those.
Dodge
Keeps fire back and escalate into bullet hell. Only the white dot on your ride can be hit. Repairs are capped each fort.
Level up
Level-ups stack behind the +LVL button (or E / gamepad Y) so they never interrupt you; spend them whenever you like. Keep rewards still open on their own. Weapons (4 slots) kill. Perks (4 slots) change how they work. Boosts are free stats. Max a weapon plus either synergy perk to evolve it.
Shop
Spend coins on relics between forts. Save coins for interest.
Field Guide
The first time you meet an enemy or mechanic a card explains it. Every card is collected under Records.

Siege
When the timer hits zero the keep marches on you, crushing your gates while hordes grow every second. Breach it within 30 seconds or you are overrun.
Ranges
Close range is the orange band within 150 of the keep: close-range weapons (Saws, Flamethrower, Orbs, Tesla, Boomerang) deal +35% there and splash 15% of their damage onto the keep, and lifesteal and armour cards only work there. Long range is behind the cyan line: long-range weapons (Storm Rod, Mortar, Missiles, Laser, Railgun) deal +25%. Engineers deploy Turrets, Drones and Mines that fight wherever they are placed.
Keeps
Every keep rolls a gate layout (Crossroads, Zigzag, High Roller, Fortified, Scatterfield...) and, from keep 2, a condition: Crosswind, Black Ice, Lava Rifts, Minefield, Blackout, Shifting Gates, Barrier Wall, Meteor Shower, Bounty Courier, Twin Keeps or Rebuilding Keep. Later keeps can stack two. Planets make matching conditions more likely.
Damage types
Weapons deal Kinetic, Explosive, Energy or Fire damage. Each faction is weak to one and resists another (shown when a keep starts and in the HUD). Armored minions fear explosives, phantoms fear energy, splitters fear fire.
Factions
Every keep is held by one of six factions (machines, bugs, beasts, demons, angels, knights) on a random planet. Each faction favours different minions.
Elites
Enemy champions fly the same rides you do and fight with the same weapons: gunships, sawmechs, interceptors, siege tanks, lancers, broodcarriers.
Grand keeps
Boss keeps creep toward you and have a mechanic: pulsing (aim for the middle), carrier (drones sweep the side lanes) or aegis (kill the shield pylons first).
